# EU AI Act: High-risk guidelines consultation
In July 2026, I contributed to a joint response to the European Commission's targeted consultation on the draft guidelines for the classification of high-risk artificial intelligence systems under Article 6 of the EU AI Act, as part of an informal AI governance working group.

## Context
The European Commission's draft guidelines provide practical guidance on the classification of AI systems as high-risk under Article 6 of the EU AI Act.

Article 6 of the EU AI Act sets out two rules for determining whether an AI system is classified as high-risk:
- Article 6(1): AI systems that are themselves products or are intended to be used as safety components of products covered by the Union harmonisation legislation listed in Annex I
- Article 6(2): AI systems falling within the high-risk use cases listed in Annex III, eligible for the Article 6(3) filter.

The consultation sought feedback on whether the draft guidelines are clear, practical and supported by examples that help AI providers and deployers assess if an AI system should be classified as high-risk.

My contributions focused on:
- identifying ambiguities in the draft guidelines
- proposing more illustrative examples to improve their practical application, and
- clarifying the application of the Article 6(3) filter to independently marketed AI components integrated into downstream high-risk systems.

The joint response was submitted to the European Commission as part of the July 2026 public consultation.

## Contributions

### 1. Detected ambiguity around the object of classification
Analysis of paragraph 48: clarification as to whether references to products "in the scope of Article 6(1)" are intended to refer to AI systems embedded in products covered by Union harmonisation legislation. 

### 2. Proposed clearer examples to illustrate the limitations in some use cases to natural persons
Analysis of paragraph 73: clearer examples to distinguish assessments of a business from evaluations of a natural person acting as a sole trader or self-employed professional. 

### 3. Suggested clarifications regarding the Article 6(3) filter and component AI systems
Analysis of paragraphs 75, 76 and 90: clarification as to how the Article 6(3) filter applies to independently marketed component AI systems whose intended purpose does not cover a downstream high-risk application. This matters because suppliers and downstream integrators may reach different concludions about whether the AI system is high-risk, depending on where in the supply chain the assessment is made.
