## Horizontal issues - Conditions for the ‘filter’ to apply

## Relevant section of the draft guidelines
**IV.2 · Horizontal issues**&nbsp;  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;**2.7. ‘Filter’ to exempt AI systems from being high-risk according to Article 6(3)**  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;**2.7.1. Conditions for the ‘filter’ to apply**  

> **(90)** Additionally, even if an AI system fulfils one of the conditions for the filter mechanism (for example, performs a narrow procedural task), such a system cannot benefit from that mechanism and will still be classified as high-risk if it forms part of a complex system where its combined intended purpose or joint outputs materially influence an individual decision within a high-risk use case or where the AI system is part of complex interconnected systems, such as agentic AI systems (see para. 72 above).

## Comment
The relationship between paragraphs 75, 76 and 90 could use further clarification. Paragraph 75 specifies that where several AI systems form part of a more complex AI system, their combined configuration is treated as a single AI system for the purposes of high-risk classification.  This appears to place the responsibility for the high-risk assessment on the provider of the integrated AI system.  

Furthermore, paragraph 76 states that AI components that are genuinely separable, put into service independently and that do not contribute to a high-risk purpose remain eligible for the Article 6(3) filter or are out of scope of the high-risk classification.   

By contrast, paragraph 90 could be interpreted as extending the high-risk classification upstream to the providers of the component AI systems, regardless of whether those systems were intended by their providers for the downstream high-risk use. For example, where an AI system is made available through an API and subsequently integrated by another provider into a complex AI system intended for a high-risk use case. 

It would be helpful to clarify whether paragraph 90 is intended to apply only to the provider of the integrated AI system as specified in paragraph 75, or also to providers of independently marketed component AI systems whose intended purpose does not cover the downstream high-risk application. This would also improve consistency with paragraph 10, which distinguishes the provider's intended purpose from reasonably foreseeable misuse.
